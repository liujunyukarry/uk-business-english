4 组迷你对话

You:Sorry, could you say that again, please?
Colleague:Of course. I said we need the figures by Thursday.
不好意思，可以再说一遍吗？——当然，我说我们周四之前需要这些数据。
You:Just to clarify, do you mean the deadline is Friday?
Manager:Yes, Friday at midday.
确认一下，截止日期是周五，对吗？——对，周五中午。
You:Can I just check one thing?
Colleague:Sure.
我可以确认一件事吗？——当然。
You:Let me summarise what we've agreed.
Manager:Go ahead.
我来总结一下我们已经达成一致的内容。——请说。

英国会议里更自然的说法

Just to clarify... 比直接说 “I don't understand” 更柔和。
Can I just check one thing? 中的 just 会让打断更礼貌。
If I understand correctly... 很适合复述后让对方确认。
Let me summarise what we've agreed. 适合会议结束前确认行动项。

容易说错的 3 个点

Repeat again. △
更自然：Could you say that again, please?
What do you mean? △
可能太直接。更柔和：When you say priority, what exactly do you mean?
I didn't understand you. △
更专业：I may have misunderstood that.
