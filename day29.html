英国常见说法 / 礼貌提醒

1. has come up
表示突然发生了需要处理的事情。
2. Would…work for you?
礼貌询问某个时间是否合适。
3. at short notice
表示临时、提前时间很短。

常见易错点

❌ I need to change the meeting time to Friday, OK?
✅ Would Friday afternoon work for you?
后者先征求对方意见。

❌ Sorry for late informing.
✅ I apologise for the short notice.
short notice 是自然的固定搭配。
