4 组迷你对话

You:Could you take me to this address, please?
Driver:Of course. It should take about twenty-five minutes.
可以送我去这个地址吗？——当然，大概需要二十五分钟。
You:Is traffic usually heavy at this time?
Driver:It can be, but I'll take the quicker route.
这个时间通常堵吗？——有时候会，不过我会走更快的路线。
You:Could you drop me at the main entrance?
Driver:No problem. It's just ahead on the left.
可以在正门让我下车吗？——没问题，就在前面左侧。
You:Do you take contactless payment?
Driver:Yes, card or contactless is fine.
You:Great. Could I have a receipt, please?
可以非接触支付吗？——可以，刷卡或非接触都行。——好的，可以给我收据吗？

英国打车里更自然的说法

motorway 是英国常用的“高速公路”，美国更常说 freeway/highway。
drop me at... 很自然，表示“让我在……下车”。
I'm due there at... 比 “I must arrive...” 更自然、更商务。
contactless 在英国非常常见，Apple Pay/银行卡感应都可这样问。

容易说错的 3 个点

Send me to this address. △
更自然礼貌：Could you take me to this address, please?
How much time? △
问路程时间更自然：How long will it take from here?
Give me an invoice. △
出租车通常先说 receipt；invoice 更像正式账单。
