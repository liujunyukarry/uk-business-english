英国常见说法 / 礼貌提醒

1. I’m responsible for…
介绍职责时比 I do… 更清楚、专业。
2. based in
表示公司或团队“位于”某地。
3. in person
强调线下当面见面。

常见易错点

❌ I am responsibility for this.
✅ I am responsible for this.
responsible 是形容词；responsibility 是名词。

❌ We are specialised on software.
✅ We specialise in software.
specialise 通常与 in 搭配。
