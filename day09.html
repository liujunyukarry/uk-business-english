You
Let me briefly introduce our company.
我先简要介绍一下我们公司。
Client
Of course. What does the company specialise in?
当然。贵公司主要专注于什么领域？

英国常见说法 / 礼貌提醒

1. I’m responsible for…
介绍职责时比 I do… 更清楚、专业。
2. based in
表示公司、办公室或团队“位于”某地。
3. in person
表示线下当面见面，适合首次拜访客户。
10 分钟后有余力再看

常见易错点

❌ I am responsibility for this.
✅ I am responsible for this.
responsible 是形容词；responsibility 是名词。

❌ We specialise on software.
✅ We specialise in software.
specialise 通常与 in 搭配。

❌ My job is coordinate the team.
✅ My role is to coordinate the team.
is 后面需要 to coordinate。
