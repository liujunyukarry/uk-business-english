英国职场礼貌提醒

1. I’m here to see…
比 I want to see… 更礼貌、更像真实前台表达。

2. 请求前台帮忙时多用 Could you…please?
英国商务场景里会显得更自然。

3. 英国通常说 lift。
美式 elevator 在英国能听懂，但 lift 更地道。

常见易错点

❌ I have a meeting with she.
✅ I have a meeting with her.

❌ Where is the elevator?
✅ Where are the lifts?

❌ Tell her I arrived.
✅ Could you let her know I’ve arrived, please?
