4 组迷你对话

You: Hi, I'm Liu. Nice to meet you.
Colleague: Nice to meet you too. Did you have a good journey over?
我也很高兴认识你。你这一路过来顺利吗？
Colleague: How long are you here for?
You: I'm here for a few days for meetings with the team.
你会在这里待多久？——我会待几天，主要和团队开会。
You: What are you working on at the moment?
Colleague: We're working on a new internal platform.
你最近在做什么？——我们在做一个新的内部平台。
You: It's great to finally put a face to the name.
Colleague: Absolutely. We've been on so many calls together!
终于见到本人了。——是啊，我们之前一起开过太多线上会议了！

英国办公室里更自然的说法

Lovely to meet you. 很英式、很自然。
at the moment = 目前、最近。
Did you have a good journey over? 很适合刚见面时问一路是否顺利。
结束寒暄：I'll let you get back to it.

容易说错的 3 个点

What are you doing now? △
问近期工作更自然：What are you working on at the moment?
How many years are you in this company? ❌
更自然：How long have you been with the company?
Nice to see you. △
第一次见面说 Nice to meet you；已经认识的人再次见面说 Nice to see you。
