4 组迷你对话

You: Hi, I have a reservation under the name Liu.
Reception: Of course. Could I see your passport, please?
当然。可以看一下您的护照吗？
You: Is breakfast included?
Reception: Yes. It's served from 6:30 to 10 on the ground floor.
是的。早餐 6:30 到 10:00 在地面层供应。
You: What time do I need to check out?
Reception: Checkout is at 11, but we can store your luggage afterwards.
11 点退房，不过之后可以帮您寄存行李。
You: The air conditioning doesn't seem to be working.
Reception: I'm sorry about that. We'll send someone up.
很抱歉，我们会派人上去查看。

英国酒店里更自然的说法

reservation / booking：两种都常见，英国日常尤其常说 booking。
ground floor：英国的地面层；再上一层才叫 first floor。
Could I... / Could you...：比直接说 “I want...” 更礼貌自然。

容易说错的 3 个点

I have booked a room. △
能用，但更自然的是：I have a reservation under the name Liu.
What's your Wi-Fi password? △
更自然：What's the Wi-Fi password?
first floor ≠ 英国的一楼
英国 ground floor 是地面层，first floor 是上一层。
