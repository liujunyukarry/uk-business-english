4 组迷你对话

You: Excuse me, could you tell me where Gate 24 is?
Staff: Certainly. Go straight ahead and turn left after security.
当然。一直往前走，过安检后左转。
You: Is it far from here?
Staff: Not really. It's about a ten-minute walk.
不算远，大概走 10 分钟。
You: Where's the taxi rank, please?
Staff: It's just outside Arrivals, on your left.
就在到达层外面，左手边。
You: I've got a connecting flight. Where should I go?
Staff: Follow the signs for Flight Connections.
跟着 Flight Connections 的标识走。

英国机场里更自然的说法

baggage reclaim：英国机场很常见；美国更常说 baggage claim。
taxi rank：英国常用，表示出租车候客区。
lift：英国英语“电梯”；美国英语常说 elevator。

容易说错的 3 个点

How many time? ❌
问“要多久”要说：How long does it take?
taxi stand → 英国更常用 taxi rank
elevator → 英国更常用 lift
